Which protocol is exactly right for me?

Standard (₹34,999): For individuals with 0 or -1 CIBIL and no formal income proof (like salary slips). We use the ₹20k RD strategy to build initial banking trust.

Verified (₹14,999): For individuals with 0 or -1 CIBIL who *have* verifiable salary or business bank statements. No RD needed.

Reset (₹16,000): For individuals with existing bad/negative CIBIL scores (defaults, late payments) that require our legal team to dispute and clean up records.

Is my Capital (₹10k Limit or ₹20k RD) safe? Who holds it?

Yes, 100% safe. Homnivas Finance does NOT hold your limit or deposit capital. It is transferred directly into an account opened in your name with an RBI-regulated Tier-1 banking partner. It remains entirely your personal asset.

Is the final ₹5 Lakh Personal Loan guaranteed?

If your profile hits the 750+ target score and you manage your credit limit flawlessly for 90 days without defaulting, you become highly pre-approved in our partner banks' systems. Final loan sanctions are subject strictly to the partner bank's underwriting policies.

Are there any hidden or subsequent charges?

Absolutely not. The Legal & Advisory Retainer fee covers the entire 90-day operation. We do not bill you further for dispute resolutions, RM support, or final loan processing.

What happens if I miss my monthly card payment?

The entire 90-day protocol relies on generating a flawless repayment track record. Missing a payment or paying late will severely damage the incubation process and result in the rejection of your final loan. You must pay strictly before due dates.

Can I apply for other apps/loans during these 90 days?

Strictly NO. Applying for other loans triggers a "Hard Inquiry" on your CIBIL profile. Multiple hard inquiries make you look desperate for credit and will crash your score instantly. You must not apply anywhere else.

How does the Reset Protocol actually remove defaults?

Our legal team acts under the Credit Information Companies Act. We file targeted disputes with bureaus to challenge inaccuracies. By law, if the creditor cannot verify the data within 30 days, the bureau must remove the negative mark.

When will I receive my Usable Credit Card?

Once your capital allocation and digital KYC are successfully verified, your account is generated instantly. The physical credit card is then dispatched by the banking partner and generally arrives at your registered address within 5 to 7 working days.

What if I don't have PAN or identity documents linked to mobile?

Identity documents linked to your active mobile number and an authentic PAN card are mandatory for the 1-minute digital Video KYC (VKYC) mandated by RBI. You cannot proceed without these documents.
