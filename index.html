Standard (₹34,999): Best for Students, Freelancers, or anyone with 0 or -1 CIBIL and zero formal income proof. Uses an RD-backed credit generation strategy.

Verified (₹14,999): For individuals with 0 or -1 CIBIL who possess verifiable salary slips or heavy business bank statements. No RD is needed here.

Reset (₹16,000): Strictly for individuals with existing bad/negative CIBIL scores, defaults, or massive inquiries that require legal dispute and aggressive cleanup.

How does the ₹20,000 RD work for Genesis Standard?

For the Standard Protocol, since you have no income proof, the bank requires an asset to issue a credit line. ₹20,000 from your initial capital is placed into a secure Recurring Deposit (RD) under your name. This RD generates standard bank interest while acting as the trust-collateral that allows the bank to issue your new credit card and report positive behavior to CIBIL.

What happens if I miss a payment or an RD installment?

Missing a credit card bill payment or an ongoing RD installment (if mandated by the bank) will instantly report negatively to CIBIL, crashing your score generation process. The 90-day protocol relies strictly on your flawless financial discipline. Homnivas will not be held liable if score targets fail due to client-side payment defaults.

How do you legally remove disputes in the Reset Protocol?

Our legal team utilizes the Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act, 2005. We file official, formatted disputes challenging inaccuracies, unauthorized hard inquiries, and duplicate default entries directly with the bureaus (CIBIL, Equifax). Bureaus have 30-45 days to resolve these. Valid disputes force the removal of negative marks, lifting your score.

Is the final ₹5 Lakh loan guaranteed? What happens on Day 91?

On Day 91, your profile transitions from "Incubation" to "Eligible". If your profile hits the 750+ target score and you managed your credit limit flawlessly, you enter the pre-approved pool. However, **final loan issuance, exact limits, and interest rates are always subject to the partner bank's final underwriting discretion** based on your verifiable cash flow and statements at that time.

What is the exact breakdown of the non-refundable fees?

The Professional/Legal Retainers (₹4,999 for Genesis, ₹6,000 for Reset) are strictly non-refundable. They cover the cost of backend server API syncs, dedicated RM salaries for 90 days, legal drafting for bureau disputes, and the final loan processing operations.
